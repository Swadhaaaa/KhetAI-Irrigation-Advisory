Under the hood

Ten purpose-built prediction models, one simple answer.

Every recommendation you see on the dashboard is generated live from these models — not looked up from a table.

💧

Next Irrigation Date & Duration

Combines soil moisture depletion rate with crop water demand to say exactly when, and for how long.

🌡️

Crop Water Requirement

Estimates daily ETc from live weather using an FAO-56 style crop-coefficient curve for sugarcane stages.

⚠️

Water Stress Probability

Scores each plot Low / Medium / High so field teams know where to act first.

🌧️

Rainfall-Adjusted Recommendation

Delays or shortens irrigation automatically when meaningful rain is forecast within 72 hours.

📉

Yield Loss Prediction

Quantifies the yield risk of pushing irrigation past the recommended date.

🧪

Fertigation Recommendation

Urea / DAP / MOP dosing tuned to crop growth stage, scaled to plot area automatically.

📈

Yield Prediction

Projects tonnes/hectare from cumulative water-stress exposure and soil health.

🔔

Alerts & Forecasting

Surfaces sensor faults, overdue irrigation and incoming weather risk before they become losses.

🗣️

Farmer-Friendly Advisory (LLM)

Converts every prediction into one short sentence, in English, Hindi, Kannada or Marathi.
