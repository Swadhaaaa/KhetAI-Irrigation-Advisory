## 8. Model Evaluation Results — Dataset Performance Only

> ⚠️ **Important distinction:**
> The metrics below describe **performance on this external model-simulated dataset**.
> They do **not** establish real-world field irrigation accuracy.
> R² is a coefficient of determination, not an "accuracy" percentage.

**Interpretation**:
- The ML model (R² = 0.8507 on original scale) significantly outperforms the mean baseline (R² = −0.0122).
- The large absolute MAE (4.23 × 10⁸ L/day) reflects the enormous scale of the simulated target values (which themselves reach 10¹⁰ L/day).
- These metrics are from a model-simulated dataset and do not imply real-world field performance.

## 10. Performance Limitation Statement

> **The model was evaluated on an external model-simulated research dataset (Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19725692). Therefore, the reported metrics describe performance on this dataset and do not establish real-world field irrigation accuracy.**
>
> - R² = 0.9421 means the model explains 94.21% of the variance in the log₁₀-transformed simulated targets — not that it is "94.21% accurate" in a field setting.
> - R² = 0.8507 on the original liters/day scale means the model explains 85.07% of the variance in the simulated target values — not real-world performance.
